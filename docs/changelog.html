iPhone app25 October 2026

Build 170

Radiators with a ruler, devices shown at once, your apps beside the chat, and reminders that get through a Focus.

What’s new

  • Radiators. Choose the temperature by scrolling a ruler in half-degree steps, with − and + at its ends for the last touch. Nothing is sent until you tap “Set to…”.
  • Devices. The list and a device's page show the last values at once, even after reopening the app; a small spinner at the top says they are being updated.
  • Apps beside the chat. In Me → Tabs, “Beside the chat” puts up to five apps to the left of the main chat (Home devices first, then the Portfolio, for example). Swipe right from the chat to reach them.
  • Time Sensitive reminders. They show on the lock screen even during a Focus or the Scheduled Summary.
  • On a phone without a box: subcategories in projects and search in sites' feeds.

What to test

  1. Open a radiator, scroll the ruler, then “Set to…” or Cancel.
  2. Put Home devices beside the chat in Me → Tabs and swipe right from the chat.
  3. Set a reminder a few minutes ahead with a Focus on.

The box

  • Install box update 0.7.8 from the app: it reads sites with an incomplete certificate (like the Mendel's), searches feeds by words and dates, has search in project categories, more readable project lists, and a file you ask Wollie for arrives in the chat you asked in. It includes 0.7.7 (recurring events, subcategories, watching feeds).
  • No new SD-card image is needed. For a new box, start with image 0.7.0, then install updates from the app.

iPhone app25 October 2026

Build 169

Wollie reads web pages itself: the text, the links to follow and the files to download.

What’s new

  • Reading the web. Give Wollie an address and it opens the page itself, reads its text and its links, and can follow them like a click. It no longer relies only on the search engine's summary, which often missed dates and numbers.
  • Files from the web. With box update 0.7.6, Wollie downloads a PDF or an attachment from a page (a school circular, for example), reads it, and can file it in a project with its category.
  • Feeds. When a site offers an RSS feed (many school sites do, for their circulars), Wollie reads its latest items in one go: the cheapest way to see what is new.
  • Safety. Only public sites are opened, never devices or pages of your home network; a scheduled job opens only the sites you allowed it.
  • On the phone without a box, Wollie reads pages too (secure https addresses), but cannot download files.

What to test

  1. Ask. “leggi https://agrariomendel.edu.it/ e dimmi le ultime circolari”. Then ask it to open one and read its PDF.
  2. Ask it to file that circular in a project, in a category.
  3. On a phone without a box, ask it to read a news page by its address.

The box

  • Install box update 0.7.6 from the app for downloading files and for the box's own page reading. It also includes 0.7.5 (Sign in with Google).
  • No new SD-card image is needed. For a new box, start with image 0.7.0, then install updates from the app.

Box update25 October 2026

Box update 0.7.7

Recurring events and real calendar editing, subcategories in projects, and a watch that follows a site's news without a model run per look.

What's new

  • Recurring events on iCloud and Google calendars: weekly on some days, every N weeks, monthly on a day or on the last Friday, yearly, until a date or a number of times. A repeating event is written on the household's clock, so it keeps its hour across summer and winter time. A first day that is not one of the repeat's days moves to the first one that is.
  • Changing an event in place (time, title, place, notes, alerts) for one occurrence or the whole series; a whole series changes its time of day, and its deleted and moved occurrences move with it. Deleting one occurrence, it and the following ones, or the series. Before, deleting one day of a series deleted all of it. Several alerts per event, and finding an event by its name.
  • Watching a feed or a page: every hour the box reads it with no model run and wakes the job the person wrote only when something new appears, handing it the new items. A new item stays waiting until a run of the job has had it, so a restart or Wollie switched off loses nothing. It reads only sites the person allowed for that job.
  • Subcategories in projects, up to three levels inside a project: “Agrario Mendel / Circolari” in Supplenze. A category page lists its subcategories, makes new ones and moves a category inside another; deleting one moves what it held up one level. Wollie names a category by its path when one name is in two places.
  • Includes 0.7.6. Wollie reads web pages and downloads files itself.

Works with any app build. This is a signed software update installed from the app. No new SD-card image is needed. For a new box, start with image 0.7.0, then install updates from the app. Images older than 0.7.0 cannot update from the app.

iPhone app21 October 2026

Build 162

See what needs your attention from any screen, with a badge on Me.

What’s new

  • Me shows the number of actions you can take: an installable box update, your own email accounts that need reconnection, and selected chat or jobs brains that need setup or access.
  • Opening Me keeps the badge until the actions are resolved. Starting an update removes that action; if it fails and can be retried, it returns. An installed update removes it.
  • Reconnecting or removing an email account, signing in to a brain, or choosing a ready one refreshes the count. A brain used by both chat and jobs counts once.
  • The badge and Me cards use the same state. Checks run while the app is active, on return and after reconnection. Old replies cannot bring back resolved warnings or data from a previous pairing.
  • Temporary network failures, usage limits that reset themselves and unused brains do not create actions. Screen-reader labels explain the count in the app’s language.

What to test

  1. With a box update available, stay in Chat: Me should show a badge. Open Me and return: the badge should remain until you start or complete the update.
  2. Resolve an owned email account’s authentication problem, test it or remove it. Its count should disappear after the box confirms the new state.
  3. Sign in to a selected brain or choose a ready one. Check that the count falls; an unused brain or a temporary usage limit should not add a badge.
  4. Put the app in the background, resolve an action from another device and return. The badge should refresh. Unpair: no box badge should remain in Solo mode.

The box

  • This is an app update. No new box release or SD-card image is needed. It uses the settings APIs supported by your box; unsupported features are skipped.
  • For a new box, start with image 0.7.0, then install box updates from the app.

Box update21 October 2026

Box update 0.7.3

Wollie can choose the useful text to copy from your request, including a draft email's subject and body, directly where it belongs in the reply.

What's new

  • All box brains receive guidance to mark self-contained copy targets according to the user's intent, without a fixed list of field types. Short values use ordinary Markdown code spans; complete drafts use text blocks that keep paragraphs and signature.
  • An email draft can offer recipient, subject and body separately. Labels, explanations and final comments stay outside the copied text. The same approach works for messages, titles, search queries and other reusable text.
  • No new API field or database migration is required. Older apps continue to receive readable Markdown replies. This is an instruction to the AI: the targets it chooses depend on the reply it generates.

Use app build 163 or later for inline highlights, copy icons, tappable text and complete draft blocks. The app also recognizes common values in older replies without repeating chips at the bottom. Solo includes the new guidance in app build 163. This is a signed software update installed from the app. No new SD-card image is needed. For a new box, start with image 0.7.0, then install updates from the app. Images older than 0.7.0 cannot update from the app.

Box update21 October 2026

Box update 0.7.2

Recover Shelly gateways after an address change, add paired radiator valves, and recognize temporary chat files before they expire. Install this software update from the app without reflashing the SD card.

What's new

  • Network discovery recognizes a known Shelly by its hardware address and offers to reconnect it at its new address. Device names and confirmation settings are preserved. Already registered devices at their current address are hidden.
  • The gateway detail in the app offers Find connected devices. Currently, Wollie imports BLU TRV radiator valves already paired in Shelly, including multiple valves per gateway. Discovery shows devices still to add or reconnect, and hides registrations that are already complete.
  • Valves retain their gateway association and can be recovered after a gateway was removed and added again. Both gateway and valve identities are checked; a temperature command checks the valve identity before sending.
  • Shelly energy meters with BTHome components and a contactor relay, including Pro EM-50, are correctly identified as meters.
  • Temporary-file warnings explain that the file has not been requested for permanent storage, show a planned deletion date and time, and include an attachment you can open. A single-file reader's description is retained for future warnings when available. Existing files may have no saved description. Warnings respect the longer lifetime of files still protected by a recent analysis session.

Use app build 161 or later for grouped gateway devices, discovery in gateway details, and previews in older file warnings. Older apps keep working. Pair new valves with their gateway in Shelly first, then add them in Wollie. For a new box, start with image 0.7.0, then install this update from the app. Images older than 0.7.0 cannot update from the app.

Calendars and home devices are set up from the app now, several appointments become one confirmation, and you can edit an earlier message and carry on from there. Your box updates from the app too, and there is a new box image to start from, 0.7.0.

Box image 0.6.0

New installation: do not start from an image older than 0.7.0. Use image 0.7.0, then install updates from the Wollie app. Earlier images cannot update from the app.

The box image for Wollie 0.6.0. Flash it with the one-line command on the site, or with Raspberry Pi Imager and wollie-repo.json.

What's new

  • Reminders. Something you have to do, with fixed words, that rings by itself at its time, without asking the AI. Alerts before it's due ("the day before at 17:00", "2 hours before"), repeats, tags, and buttons on the notification to mark it done or snooze it. Today's are on the app's Today tab.
  • Portfolio. Accounts, made of wallets read from their chain or amounts you type for an exchange, with a balance chart in dollars, euros or bitcoin and how it splits. Ten chains now: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Polygon, BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Solana, Sui and IoTeX, and a 0x address is read on every one of those that uses it. Each coin has a Market section, and the box re-reads the wallets every hour.
  • Signed requests. At home the app talks to the box over plain HTTP. With app build 147 or later, every request is now signed with a key that stays on the phone, so someone on your Wi-Fi can no longer act as you. It does not hide the traffic: the privacy page says exactly what is and isn't covered.
  • Emails you can correct on the confirmation card. You can change the subject and text before the email goes. The recipients can't be changed there.
  • A general off switch for the box's owner, in the console and in the app. Off means no AI calls and no scheduled jobs; reminders still ring.
  • Scheduled jobs follow any calendar rule, on the household's clock, and a run missed while the box was off is made up.
  • The brain can be Claude or, to experiment, an endpoint of your own that speaks the same API.
  • The box's Claude command-line tool sends no telemetry, error reports or update checks.

A box you already have: there is no in-place update yet, and flashing this image starts a box from scratch. If your box is in use, it is fine to leave it on its version for now. Phones on app builds before 147 keep working; once a phone has opened 147, don't go back to an older build, or it will need pairing again.
