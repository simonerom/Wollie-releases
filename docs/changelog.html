iPhone app18 October 2026

Build 156

Calendars and home devices are set up from the app now, several appointments become one confirmation, and you can edit an earlier message and carry on from there. Your box updates from the app too, and there is a new box image to start from, 0.7.0.

What's new

  • Me, Calendars. Connect iCloud or another calendar with an app-specific password (the box tries it before saving it), see what is inside, remove it. The box's owner decides whether Wollie asks before writing.
  • Several appointments at once (“add these four exam dates”) are one confirmation, with a tick per event: untick the ones you do not want.
  • Me, Home devices. Your Shelly plugs, switches and valves, whether they answer, look for new ones on the network, add, rename, remove. Wollie can put the question back before switching a device, never take it away.
  • Long press one of your messages: “Edit and continue from here”. What came after it disappears and Wollie picks up from the edited message; “Undo” brings everything back. “Reply” quotes a precise message, so Wollie knows what you are answering.
  • A job that could not run (a usage limit, no internet) leaves a message with “Try again”. A photo or file you sent in the chat that is about to be deleted leaves one with “Keep it”.
  • After you confirm something, the chat says “You confirmed” and what, instead of a technical line.
  • Word, OpenDocument and RTF files are read like PDFs.
  • Me tab. “Box version”, with “Check now”; the box's owner updates it with one tap and sees each step. “Add another device” shows a code and a QR for your iPad or another phone.
  • A job's result or a reminder shows up in the chat while you look at it. The context line has “compact” on the left and “new conversation” on the right.
  • With the brain on a compatible endpoint, the jobs that search the web run on Claude when the box also has a Claude key.

What to try

  1. Me, Calendars. Connect a calendar, then ask Wollie to add two appointments at once.
  2. Long press a message of yours, “Edit and continue from here”, change it and send; then “Undo”.
  3. Long press a reply of Wollie's, “Reply”, and ask about that one.

The box

  • Image 0.7.0 is the one to flash on a new box: from then on it updates from the app. A box flashed with an earlier image cannot update from the app.

Box image17 October 2026

Box image 0.7.0

A new box image, and the same version as an update for boxes already on 0.6.1: the box's owner sees it on the Me tab and taps Update now. Wollie is offline for about a minute while the box restarts, and your data stays where it is.

What's new

  • Calendars from the app (Me, Calendars): connect iCloud or another CalDAV calendar with an app-specific password, which the box tries before it saves it. The box's owner decides whether Wollie asks before writing.
  • Several appointments at once are one confirmation with a tick per event; only the ticked ones are written. Times are read on the household's clock, summer or winter time for that date.
  • Home devices from the app (Me, Home devices): your Shelly devices, whether they answer, a search of the network, add, rename, remove. The question before switching a device is the owner's: Wollie can put it back, never take it away.
  • Edit an earlier message and carry on from there, with Undo; reply to a specific message.
  • A job that could not run leaves a message with Try again; a file sent in the chat that is about to be deleted leaves one with Keep it.
  • Word, OpenDocument and RTF files are read like PDFs.
  • A box update shows each step as it happens; another device of yours is added with a code and a QR from the app.
  • Under the hood. The fixes from an adversarial review of all of the above.

Install image 0.7.0 on a new box: from then on it updates from the app. A box on the 0.6.0 image or earlier can't update in place. The new screens need the Wollie app build 156 or later; older apps keep working with this box.

Box image11 October 2026

Box update 0.6.1

The first update for the box that installs from the app: the box's owner sees it on the Me tab and taps Update now. Wollie is offline for about a minute while the box restarts, and your data stays where it is.

What's new

  • Several chats. Start a new one for each topic, and Wollie names it after your first message.
  • Scheduled jobs can search the web when their web switch is on, and open pages only on the sites you allow with a tap.
  • Verified senders. An attachment from a trusted sender downloads by itself only when the sender's own mail provider confirms who sent it. Anything else asks you first.
  • Box updates from the app. When a new version is ready, the owner installs it with one tap. If the new version doesn't start, the box goes back by itself to how it was before the update.
  • Under the hood. Dependencies pinned with hashes, a sturdier way to ship code to the box, and many fixes from code reviews.

Boxes on the 0.6.0 image or earlier can't install this update in place: they get these changes with the next image. The Update card needs the Wollie app build 152 or later; older apps keep working with this box.

iPhone app10 October 2026

Build 150

Several chats, one per topic: swipe sideways to go from one to the next. And a reading mode for long answers.

What's new

  • Chats. Tap “New chat” and you are in it straight away; Wollie names it from your first message, and you can rename it any time. An empty new chat you have not written in is reopened instead of making another.
  • Swipe sideways to go from one chat to the next; the dots under the name say which one you are in, and which one is answering or has a new reply. Rename, reorder and delete chats from the list (tap the name at the top).
  • Wollie has the same memories in every chat, but each chat only sees its own messages. Reports, notices and Telegram arrive in the main chat.
  • Reading mode. Long-press a message and choose “Reading mode” (or open it from Quick settings). Full screen, an easy typeface, seven sizes, white, sepia or night page.
  • Scroll back through a chat and a search bar comes up at the bottom: type and press Search.
  • Scheduled jobs can search the web when their “Search the web” switch is on (on the job's page). Wollie turns it on when a job needs it; with the web on, sending mail, changing the calendar or controlling a device always asks for a tap.
  • The icons at the top sit on the right, and the cost is no longer there (it is in Settings).
  • “Tidy above” also takes a number of your own (“Another number…”).

What to try

  1. Tap “Wollie” at the top, tap “New chat” and ask something: the chat gets a name with the answer.
  2. Long-press an answer, choose “Reading mode”, then “Aa” to change size and page.
  3. Scroll up in a chat, type a word in the bar at the bottom and press Search.

Box image3 October 2026

Box image 0.6.0

The box image for Wollie 0.6.0. Flash it with the one-line command on the site, or with Raspberry Pi Imager and wollie-repo.json.

What's new

  • Reminders. Something you have to do, with fixed words, that rings by itself at its time, without asking the AI. Alerts before it's due ("the day before at 17:00", "2 hours before"), repeats, tags, and buttons on the notification to mark it done or snooze it. Today's are on the app's Today tab.
  • Portfolio. Accounts, made of wallets read from their chain or amounts you type for an exchange, with a balance chart in dollars, euros or bitcoin and how it splits. Ten chains now: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Polygon, BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Solana, Sui and IoTeX, and a 0x address is read on every one of those that uses it. Each coin has a Market section, and the box re-reads the wallets every hour.
  • Signed requests. At home the app talks to the box over plain HTTP. With app build 147 or later, every request is now signed with a key that stays on the phone, so someone on your Wi-Fi can no longer act as you. It does not hide the traffic: the privacy page says exactly what is and isn't covered.
  • Emails you can correct on the confirmation card. You can change the subject and text before the email goes. The recipients can't be changed there.
  • A general off switch for the box's owner, in the console and in the app. Off means no AI calls and no scheduled jobs; reminders still ring.
  • Scheduled jobs follow any calendar rule, on the household's clock, and a run missed while the box was off is made up.
  • The brain can be Claude or, to experiment, an endpoint of your own that speaks the same API.
  • The box's Claude command-line tool sends no telemetry, error reports or update checks.

A box you already have: there is no in-place update yet, and flashing this image starts a box from scratch. If your box is in use, it is fine to leave it on its version for now. Phones on app builds before 147 keep working; once a phone has opened 147, don't go back to an older build, or it will need pairing again.
